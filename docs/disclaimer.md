# 9.  In Closing

The main goal of this guideline is to provide useful pedagogical suggestions and recommendations. In particular, the guideline strives to:

- recognize existing practices and initiatives in one place,
- create a space for pedagogical knowledge sharing among faculty, and
- offer a language for students to understand their curriculum design.

Please keep the guideline authors informed of your latest pedagogical endeavors, so that we can help celebrate critical and creative works of students and faculty on the SUTD campus.
