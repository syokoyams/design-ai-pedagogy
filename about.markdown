# About

This website publishes the **Guidelines for Design·AI Education**, prepared by the Design·AI Education Task Force at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD).

The guidelines translate the SUTD Design·AI Education framework into actionable guidance for faculty designing their courses and for students seeking to understand the design rationale behind their curriculum. As a living document, it will be updated regularly to reflect feedback from SUTD students and faculty, emerging pedagogical best practices, and new Design·AI education initiatives across campus.

For enquiries, please contact the co-chairs of the Design·AI Education Task Force:
