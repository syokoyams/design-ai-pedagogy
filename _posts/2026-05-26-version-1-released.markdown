# Version 1.0 Released

*26 May 2026*

The inaugural version of the Guidelines for Design·AI Education (ver. 1) is now live. This document translates the SUTD Design·AI Education framework into actionable guidance for faculty designing their courses and for students seeking to understand the design rationale behind their curriculum.

This is a living document and will be updated regularly to reflect feedback from SUTD students and faculty, as well as emerging pedagogical best practices.
